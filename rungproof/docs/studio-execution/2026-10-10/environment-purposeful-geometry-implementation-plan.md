# All-77 purpose-specific geometry implementation plan

**Proposed geometry contracts, not implementation or acceptance.** Existing C# art-direction ledger stays frozen. This plan addresses the repeated two-wall room problem across all 77 scenes, including all 24 bench lessons. A unique name, hue, roof option or window option does not satisfy it.

## What the lesson does and what the setting means

For bench-only lessons the authored plant is the instructional button/lamp/display station. Those logic exercises do not specify 24 different physical factories. Their settings below are explicitly illustrative teaching locations, selected to express one operator, paired independent roles, shared confirmation, commissioning, or numerical instrumentation. They must not introduce fabricated functional machinery, safety claims or sensor feedback. Different rooms are a product art direction, not a claim about PLC semantics.

Machine and process scenes instead use their actual conveyors, lift elevations, tank/pit/water, robot motion, loading/takeaway axes and field installations to define credible service-building adjacency. Several scenes can belong to one facility while presenting materially different local footprints and relationships. No unrelated object is added merely to make a signature unique.

## Geometric reference and hard boundaries

- B is the existing protected authored equipment plus declared motion and mounted-control standing-access envelope. Do not shrink B or move equipment to accommodate art.
- W and D are the expanded context-frame width/depth outside B; H is declared human-scale wall height. Ratios below define actual architectural extents, not camera yaw or normalized equipment motion.
- Rear and side edges remain WORLD-axis lines selected from the current rear-corner signs. Outward means away from B on that edge. All wing projections, cheek walls, canopies and supports stay outside B with the existing positive clearance margin.
- A detached wing is a grounded small building mass with contiguous foundation and supported top, not a floating isolated wall. U/L shapes are constructed outward behind the boundary so no cheek wall intrudes into process or operator access.
- Leave the current camera-facing standing approach open. Do not add accessible rear stock, tools or destinations to bench settings. Context service openings are illustrative; no walking/reach/compliance clearance is asserted.
- Front and side cutaways must not hide mounted heads. Roof surfaces stay over outward annex/canopy bands; do not place a roof over the authored plant, tank radar or open robot volume.
- Keep original roads, water, pits and floor datum. New mineral aprons do not overwrite those features. No stripes, repeated grids, tile seams or random line textures.
- Context remains passive/unbound with no collider, process controller, I/O, pick binding or Reset ownership. Original procedural/owned assets only; functional KIN/LENS geometry and colors remain authored.

## Implementation structure

Replace each exact LayoutVariant's generic two-wall call with a small explicit shell composer. Reuse low-level grounded wall, aperture, annex wing, boundary, supported canopy and courtyard primitives. Make the feature contract in this table visible through real wall footprint, absence of enclosure, separate building mass, stepped height or process-specific adjacency. Optional material/trim differences come after the physical form.

A primitive can be shared without producing the same finished scene. Use exact per-scene wall intervals, wing offsets/lengths, low/high extents, opening orientation and connection graph. Do not fall back to the old two-wall room for a missed key; record a missing implementation and stop visual acceptance for that scene. Existing art-direction values remain material/proportion inputs, not substitutes for these forms.

Every new portal is a real hole with clear jambs, no window glazing across the opening and no post/beam occupying its interior. Every roof has real outside supports. Native static envelope checks remain bounded; they are not physical or continuous safety proof.
