## Common construction gate

Each indoor location must be a connected whole envelope: rear wall joins its world-axis return at the measured corner; a service vestibule/recess shares a real foundation, return and supported roof. Camera-facing cutaways retain all functional bodies, mounted heads and operator standing zones. Outdoors must be complete sites with credible grounded building adjacency and connected ground, rather than an indoor room transplanted outside. A backing wall in a loading recess is explicitly a cutaway service space, not a certified through-loading route.

An exact layout case remains mandatory, but case coverage is not quality. Retain the protected equipment/motion/control-access bounds. All added vertical context stays outside those bounds and existing margins. Personnel openings are at least1.15m wide and2.2m high, with clear jambs; low partition gaps become nonperson open-top notches and must not receive personnel portal classification. No glass, posts, floor thresholds or generic furniture may cross an opening. All outward masses have a foundation/apron; all roof members have grounded support/contact. Materials use quiet nonperiodic grain; no grids, stripes or panel-line background overlays.

## Present risks

The current75 draft layouts contain fragmented wall/U/wing variants and low nominal service openings that have not been accepted as whole places. Complete room proportions must remain visible in the actual teaching camera; detailed offscreen architecture is wasted. Large distributed machinery needs enough visible midground context without expanding a dark empty ground plane. Passive fixtures can erase context or block service entries despite lying outside the process AABB, so their circulation and sightlines require actual review. Static geometry checks are not continuous-motion, physical-safety or ergonomic certification.
