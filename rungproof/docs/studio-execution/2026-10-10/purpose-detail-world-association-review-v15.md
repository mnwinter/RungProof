Source JSON declares north_call_button atX−2.4 and south_call_button atX+2.4, with distinct toggle actions, explicit NORTH CALL/SOUTH CALL faces and north_call_pressed/south_call_pressed PC bindings. Source JSON coordinates are not final mounted control coordinates.

`DemoEnvironmentPresentation.cs:56` derives rearX from camera direction: positive cameraX gives sx−1. `TeachingSuites.cs:165–177` creates two bay centers cu±1.65 and labels the lower **u** North. Its At transform maps u→sx*u. Thus North is assigned the positive-worldX bay, opposite the North control's relative ordering; South occupies the negative-worldX bay. Actual PNG shows rear SOUTH left/NORTH right while foreground labels are NORTH left/SOUTH right. This is not a camera-only problem and must not be solved by screen-coordinate swapping.

Preferred measured repair: supply the already-composed, immutable world positions of exact north_call_button and south_call_button to passive environment construction. Convert these to environment-local/world-axis X, sort the two board bay world-X coordinates and assign exact station IDs by matching the controls' world-X order (one-to-one), with source guards for scene ID, action IDs and exact point bindings. Preserve existing bay walls, recesses, head positions, tags and runtime. Move or reassign only passive board identities/text (including board node names/metadata). Reject equal/ambiguous X ordering rather than guessing. A fixed exact-scene correction comparing sx*bayCenter against sx*cu would repair today's orientation, but an explicit measured-control contract better survives future packing/layout changes.

Acceptance: compare named board centers against associated retained controls in world coordinates, test both room mirror signs, then actual normal/purpose/operator views. Verify no North/South cross-association and preserve actual North/South input actions and shared beacon behavior. Neither control needs relocation.
