## Retained limits

Floor/plaster microtexture remains subtle at wide framing, particularly in split. Existing wood grain is much more apparent. Hard shadows remain strong; some dark bench feet merge locally with their shadow. Several wall plaques are clipped by the split aperture, and ordinary overview physical text is often too small to read. These are explicit framing/readability limits, not proof of new material defects or of comfortable readability. No global lighting/exposure, device materials or simulation behavior was qualified by this review.
