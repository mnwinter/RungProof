# Dot review: distinct purposeful locations

Status: new visual standard active; historical 154 catalog frames are baseline only. This document is a proposal review gate, not approval of unseen implementation.

## Grounded reason for rejection

SceneEnvironmentProfiles.cs currently assigns 77 scenes to eight families with repeated variants and accent colors. DemoEnvironmentPresentation.Catalog.cs begins with common concrete, off-white room materials, minimum room dimensions and two closing sides. TrainingWorkshop returns after the common shell; its supported station is separately generated by SceneComposer.ControlInstallations.cs with the same top/frame/backboard materials and arrangement formula. The controls installation improved credibility but did not establish distinct locations. A new family name, hue or decorative prop does not resolve that structural repetition.

## Required explicit 77-row direction

Every catalog ID needs a purpose, facility/location, architectural silhouette, plan arrangement, equipment-to-location relationship, operator approach, service/access destination, material purpose, lighting source and camera-visible differentiator. Record protected authored geometry and ground exceptions. Shared modules are sensible; incidental random choices are not art direction.

Each scene must differ from its closest catalog sibling in at least two noncolor structural dimensions: enclosure/open edges; openings and their adjacency; roof/support silhouette; circulation/service-zone placement; wall/facade organization; believable process-support context. These dimensions must be visible in the actual teaching frames. Numerical uniqueness, altered sign text, small windows, tint or stock replacement alone fails. Distinction must make sense for the lesson, not invent a different process.

Student controls can share credible standard hardware. Different lesson stations should read as purposeful arrangements in distinct alcoves, paired inspection stations, commissioning bays or bench orientations/adjacencies. Preserve successful supported heads, wiring duct and clear bench frontage. Do not disguise identical rooms by putting unrelated industry props behind a bench.

## Early representative gate

Review six substantially contrasting full/split pairs before broader rollout:
- Lab2.1 apprentice materials-call alcove.
- Lab2.8 paired inspection station: must demonstrate a meaningful difference from Lab2.1, not just a different beacon.
- Lab2.15 extraction/maintenance bay with credible visible service context.
- Lab4.8 dispatch hall with clear operator approach and loading destination.
- Lab11.6 open wastewater utility yard preserving authored vessels/ground.
- Lab6.8 public-service washroom with appropriate washable materials and connected wall installation.

A food washdown hall and urban crossing can be additional targeted checks. Root selects final capture order. A common beige backdrop across these is a blocking failure even if every native hierarchy test passes.

## Actual render acceptance

Inspect each scene and split image individually with exact scene ID, assembly identity and SHA-256. Inspect the largest visible viewport areas, not just a new prop. Architecture must show depth, coherent room/yard boundaries and process orientation. Distant or offscreen work does not count.

No wall, support, roof or decor may obscure controls, displays, process feedback, field motion corridor or the visible standing approach. Door destinations require visible route continuity; storage cannot demand access through equipment. Camera correction must frame the installation, including the already rejected XY cabinet. A static image supports visible-route judgment only, not physical clearance.

Materials should explain use: washable surfaces for food/washroom, metal structure for logistics, masonry/service finishes for maintenance, outdoor concrete/weathered utility surfaces for yards. Use restrained nonrepeating texture detail and varied material response. No ruled/grid/striped backgrounds. Color variations must not alter functional lamp or safety-state meanings. Lighting should show credible sources, dimensional contrast and legible controls without washed-out walls or black voids.

Context that looks connected must be truthful. A cylinder beside a fan is not an extraction duct. Decorative cables do not establish electrical design. Keep additions noninteractive and distinguish them from authored controls.

## Technical preservation and final coverage

Environment nodes remain presentation siblings with no collisions, runtime or point bindings. Preserve equipment IDs, KIN names/rest poses, tags, authored programs, model semantics and pick targets. Retain authored pits, water/roads and ground datum. Verify lighting/material/floor restoration after navigation and removal. Rooms stay world-axis aligned to process, not camera rotation.

After the representative gate, all77 require new scene/split inspection and per-scene findings; previous catalog evidence cannot be inherited. Re-run focused existing structural, control pick/press/selector/lamp/reset and authored-contract checks against frozen source. New screenshots do not imply continuous motion clearance, real electrical/physical safety, minimum GPU, accessibility or distribution approval.

## Approval disposition

Proposed 12 architecture families and explicit scene directions are conditionally suitable as reusable construction tools. Reject them if implementation becomes 12 fixed repeated rooms. Matrix and actual representative renders remain pending. No broad visual PASS is issued by this report.
