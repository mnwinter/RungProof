# Ten-zone diagnostic capture and compact drive revision

Status: source implemented; fresh coordinated compile/native/render still required.

The opt-in `--capture-tenzone-state=` diagnostic supports all-running, blocked-downstream, final-beam-stop and invalid-current. It writes an explicitly named temporary QA project under `.tools`, opens it through the actual shell project-open/validation/load handler, invokes current-draft Run, accepts the existing request action and advances accepted 20 ms scans. It holds the automatic physics clock and labels that hold visibly. The final sensing-line case uses physical occupancy on the next scan to stop all ten commands. The invalid-current case opens an invalid current draft and confirms old execution stops and monitor identity is false. These are diagnostic reference projects, not authored learner starter solutions or product autoload behavior.

Evidence logging records project/source/loaded-program hashes, current editor identity, actual accepted scan number, inputs, outputs, physical carton/beam state, shaft/drum transforms and per-zone belt UV offsets. The capture tool must wait for `TENZONE_CAPTURE_READY state=...` before accepting an image.

Only Lab10.6 drives are now uniformly 0.55 times their original scale. Their actual scaled shaft center is aligned to the unchanged Y1.20 drum axis before their foot datum is measured. The actual shaft tip is placed at Z0.60 against the drum end Z0.53. Pad top equals measured original foot bottoms; 60 mm pad thickness and 30 mm floor feet determine the grounded leg height. Actual shaft bounds determine the coupling and illustrative reduction housing center. Lab10.4/10.5 remain unchanged. This is a geared teaching illustration, not motor/gearbox hardware sizing.
