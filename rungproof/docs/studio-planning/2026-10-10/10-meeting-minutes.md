## Proceedings and disagreements

Architecture corrected its own visible-reset trial alternative: an isolated pure worker leaves live exploration intact, while historical evidence is separately labeled. Product identified missing starter/glossary/policy and a pilot input variation outside the disclosed pulse contract. UX challenged cancellation cleanup, document access, callback ownership and assistive claims. Art separated model coordinates from rendered installation scale and visual approval from rights. QA required real negative-case discrimination/native parity and canceled-delivery races. Production separated tranche cost/scope from campaign and public delivery, and named operating gate owners.

Root independently flagged exact binding aliases, shadow task/network eligibility, copied-versus-read-only maps, controller semantic identity, worker multiplication, viewport ambiguity, and concurrent baseline drift. No department's conclusion was treated as runtime/native/security/rights proof.
