## Acceptance additions

Capture exact-artifact native idle, started/released Start, beam crossed, following-scan inhibited, ordinary Stop, simultaneous inputs, blocked restart and Reset states. Recorded copied scan observations support signal correctness; screenshots support framing/readability only. Evaluate long tags, compact split, hints/results open and supported DPI using actual content bounds. Camera moves/resize must not cause picking/input leakage into ladder controls. Monochrome and muted operation retain evidence. No optional camera shake, automatic orbit, flashing celebration or sound; modeled carton travel remains truthfully visible during ordinary exploration.

Current runtime sensor hold can leave feedback true briefly after geometric crossing; Learning must describe sampled/post-advance phases without deriving feedback solely from apparent overlap. Do not alter motion or sensor values to make screenshots look successful. Native bounds assertions alone cannot close this gate or prove physical clearance.

Provenance manifest includes each shipped mesh/texture/SVG/font hash, source/creator/reference, rights/notice evidence and visual review artifact. Existing brand SVGs remain canonical. Generic reference notes or historical geometry review do not establish redistribution permission. Rights owner reports unresolved evidence separately; internal pilot authorization cannot silently become public release authorization.
