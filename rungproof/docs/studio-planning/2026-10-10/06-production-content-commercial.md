## Content authoring and review pipeline

1. Intake: challenge ID/revision, audience/prerequisites, learning outcome, exact scene/version and retained demo association. Use one versioned content source; generated help/catalog entries are projections.
2. Controls contract: symbolic bindings, command/feedback/permissive distinction, input stimulus ownership, scan period/order, realistic limitations, Stop/Reset semantics, allowed instruction set. Exact vendor equivalence and protective claims excluded without independent evidence.
3. Learning packet: disclosed objective and cases, starter draft, tag glossary, sequential hints, optional worked explanation and diagnostic vocabulary. Reference solution is one example, never grading oracle.
4. Author tests: two valid structural solutions with renamed-tag variants and defect fixtures. Controls reviews intended behavior; independent QA authors or challenges negative cases so author/evaluator agreement is not the only evidence.
5. Engine integration: validate bounded schema/data predicates, content identity and copied phase-correct observations; native parity confirms the same plant and output mapping.
6. Art/accessibility review: exact relevant asset inventory, candidate/review contradictions resolved, labels/camera/contrast/noncolor evidence, keyboard path and assistive scope tested on actual renderer. Recognition review remains separate from rights review.
7. Editorial/claims review: source-backed portable instruction education, accurate model semantics and known limitations, no misleading hardware labels; regenerate only from proper source and review changes to generated help.
8. Fresh artifact QA: challenge/version/evaluator/plant/program identity in manifest; draft/recovery/cancel/stale status; supported demo preservation; exact package workflows.
9. Pilot, revise and approve: record observed behavior, hints and first-failure comprehension; update content revision and invalidate obsolete current proof explicitly.
10. Release register: supported scope, reviewed assets/rights/notices, content approvers, artifact hash and rollback version. Changes to semantics/assets/text that affect grading or teaching reopen the appropriate gate.

Do not authorize full-catalog conversion or asset portfolio expansion as part of the first slice. Instructors may browse experimental scenes only under accurate limitations; externally advertised campaign includes only individually approved content.

## Exact pilot proposal and gates

Propose six participants after M3 and separately approved recruitment: four intended controls learners unfamiliar with RungProof and two experienced controls practitioners unfamiliar with its UI. Experienced participants assess semantics; do not pool their task speed with novices. Observe six individual sessions of up to 45 minutes, with consent and local anonymized notes. No unsolicited emails/contacts and no automatic telemetry. Record build/content identity, prior ladder experience, objective comprehension, process-vs-toolbar Stop confusion, first valid test time, hints, failures, intervention and ability to explain feedback latency.

Each participant attempts the same single challenge from a fresh local profile; permit self-selected built-in hints; do not coach unless required to recover a blocked session, recording intervention. Ask participant to explain why the sensor stopped motion and what Reset preserves. A transfer probe changes the disclosed Start pulse duration within the contract, not hidden new machine behavior.

Provisional product gate: at least 3/4 novice participants complete within 30 minutes without facilitator intervention (built-in hints allowed), at least 3/4 explain command vs feedback and Stop priority, and both experienced reviewers find no unreconciled controls-semantic contradiction. Zero unresolved critical data-loss, false-pass or offline-boundary defect; no observed participant remains blocked by a required control with no accessible alternative. Time targets are design targets, not established educational norms. Report all six results individually, tiny sample limits and concrete revisions. Failure triggers a bounded revision and a proposed fresh 4-novice follow-up, requiring participant/time approval; no endless testing inferred. Success permits discussion of next campaign tranche, not public launch.
