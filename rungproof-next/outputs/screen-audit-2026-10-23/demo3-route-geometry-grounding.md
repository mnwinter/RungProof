## Authored decision and trustworthy native scope

At `part_at_pusher=True`, `carton_height_mm` reports the current sequence height. The LAD latches side divert only for **height >600**, so 420 and the inclusive 600 boundary follow main, 820 follows side (`AuthoredDemoLadderPrograms.cs:214–245`). Conveyor request requires machine_run, no divert and pusher_retracted; pusher request requires machine_run/divert/part at station. Distinct conveyor/pusher FB calls own their outputs (262–267). Height becomes **0 off station**, so a later zero-height frame cannot identify the discharged carton by itself (`SceneSimulationRuntime.cs:1108–1109`). Retain chronological height evidence.
