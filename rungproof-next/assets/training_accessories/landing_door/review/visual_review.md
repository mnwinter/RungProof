# Personnel-door enclosure panel visual review

Review date: 2026-09-22

Disposition: **independent recognition passed - eligible for strict production gate**

## Asset and I/O boundary

This static enclosure panel has no declared external I/O. It is not evidence of
a safety-rated guard, door interlock, fire rating, access-control function,
egress compliance, installation approval, or live-machine safety function.

## Independent acceptance

A source-blind reviewer identified an insulated industrial wall/partition module
with a personnel access door at 0.94 confidence and accepted it for simulator
use. The visible door slab, frame, handle, kick plate, wall cladding, base rail,
and top cap establish the intended family.

The red sign panel is intentionally blank, the hinges and latch are simplified,
the exposed insulation edge is a stylized construction cue, and the kick plate
is generic. Those limitations do not establish any safety or access claim.
