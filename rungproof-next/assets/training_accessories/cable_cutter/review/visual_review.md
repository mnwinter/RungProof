# Ladder cable tray visual review

Review date: 2026-09-22

Disposition: **independent recognition passed - eligible for strict production gate**

## Asset and I/O boundary

This is a static factory-kit segment with no declared reusable external I/O.
It does not claim cable-fill capacity, conductor rating, grounding/bonding,
support spacing, splice specification, installation approval, or compliance
with a cable-tray standard.

## Independent acceptance

The previous review correctly rejected ambiguous black geometry that appeared to
cross the cable bundle or float beside the tray. The accepted revision leaves
only continuous parallel rails, evenly exposed transverse rungs, equal inset
cable ends, and two visibly connected under-tray yokes. A source-blind
reviewer identified a ladder-style cable tray carrying routed power/control
cables at 0.96 confidence and accepted it as a reusable straight simulator
segment.

The cable routing remains deliberately orderly and idealized. It omits bends,
entry/exit routes, fill constraints, hangers, splice plates, grounding bonds,
and fastening hardware; those must be represented by scene-specific equipment
when needed, not inferred from this asset.
