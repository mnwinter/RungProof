# M18 inductive proximity sensor visual review

Review date: 2026-09-22

Disposition: **independent recognition passed - eligible for strict production gate**

## Asset and I/O boundary

The catalog's symbolic `detected` output is simulator-only. This model does not
claim real sensing distance, target material response, switching behavior,
wiring standard, safety function, or electrical rating.

## Independent acceptance

The initial review rejected an obscured generic panel sensor. A second review
rejected a visible blue beam because inductive sensing is not optical. The final
revision exposes the threaded barrel, locknuts/washers, sensing face, connector,
cable, and metallic target without an optical cue. A source-blind reviewer
identified a threaded inductive proximity sensor at 0.86 confidence and passed
it for recognition-level simulator use. Sensing-face, gap, connector, and
retention detail remain polish items.
