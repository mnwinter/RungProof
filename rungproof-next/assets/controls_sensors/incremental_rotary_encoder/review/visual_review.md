# Incremental rotary encoder visual review

Disposition: **independent recognition passed - eligible for strict production gate**

The flange, bolt pattern, encoder body, and projecting shaft clearly identify a rotary encoder. Incremental behavior is a catalog and simulator contract, not a visual claim: the render does not establish resolution, channel phasing, output technology, count direction, or absolute-versus-incremental operation. The symbolic outputs are `position_counts` and `speed_rpm`.
