# M30 capacitive proximity sensor visual review

Review date: 2026-09-22

Disposition: **independent recognition passed - eligible for strict production gate**

## Asset and I/O boundary

The catalog's symbolic `detected` output is simulator-only. This model does not
claim real sensing distance, target-material response, switching behavior,
wiring standard, safety function, electrical rating, or any manufacturer
configuration.

## Independent acceptance

Earlier evidence read only as a generic wired sensor because a large mounting
plate hid the barrel, the target looked like a panel, and the capacitive subtype
had no visible identity cue. The accepted revision exposes the threaded M30
barrel, paired hex locknuts, black sensing face, M12-style cable termination,
green status indicator, marked adjustment/nameplate details, and a separate
open plastic hopper containing non-metallic pellets. A source-blind reviewer
identified a likely threaded M30 capacitive proximity sensor at 0.89 confidence
and passed it for recognition-level industrial-simulator use.

The reviewer noted that the white nameplate marking is small, the sensing face
is partly oblique in the hero view, the illustrated target gap is not a stated
range, and the hopper is stylized. Those are presentation limits, not a claim
of electrical or process performance.
