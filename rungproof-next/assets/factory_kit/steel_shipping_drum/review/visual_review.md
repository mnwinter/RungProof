Review date: 2026-09-22

Disposition: **independent recognition passed - eligible for strict production gate**

## Asset boundary

This is a static generic closed-head steel drum. It represents visible bulk
storage or transport hardware only; it does not identify contents, hazard
class, pressure rating, sealing state, chemical compatibility, inspection
status, or shipping compliance.

## Source corrections

- Replaced four bright, equally prominent body rings with proportionate
  body-coloured rolling hoops and distinct top/bottom chimes.
- Added a rolled top seam, two hexagonal closure assemblies with collars and
  recesses, and a neutral blank placard area. The placard deliberately has no
  product, hazard, or handling information.

## Independent acceptance

The first source-blind review rejected the initial model because its bright,
repeated rings and flat lid resembled a stylized pressure vessel. After the
source correction, a separate source-blind reviewer identified a generic
closed-head industrial steel drum at 0.90 confidence and returned a PASS for
normal simulator use. The reviewer noted that the blank placard projects from
the shell, the drum is pristine and glossy, the bung details remain stylized,
and its ribs are slightly prominent. The blank placard is never evidence of
contents, hazards, or asset identity.
