## Independent acceptance

A source-blind reviewer identified a horizontal wide-flange steel structural
beam/I-beam assembly at 0.93 confidence and accepted it for professional
industrial-simulator use. The reviewer noted that the beam-end profile is not
fully exposed and that connection, weld, and surface detail are deliberately
simplified. Those limitations do not compromise normal simulator recognition;
they do preclude construction-specific training claims.
